# Home artwork image provenance

The three homepage artworks are public-domain paintings by Vincent van Gogh. The source files were donated by The Metropolitan Museum of Art to Wikimedia Commons under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required, but the artist and collection are identified here and in the homepage caption.

The repository copies were decoded from the Wikimedia originals, converted to sRGB JPEG, resized so the longest edge is 1280 px or less, and saved with progressive JPEG encoding. No content outside the artwork was cropped.
